Privacy Policy

Command.Crew — last updated July 2026

Summary

Command.Crew is a restaurant order-taking app for waiters. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

How the app works

Command.Crew connects only to the restaurant's own admin system over the restaurant's local WiFi network. Order data (tables, menu items, orders) is exchanged directly between the operator's phone and the restaurant's own admin device — it never leaves that local network or reaches our servers.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate any backend service that receives data from this app.

Third-party services

Command.Crew does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
