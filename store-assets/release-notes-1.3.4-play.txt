What's new in 1.3.4

- A billed table opens straight to its bill: take payment and print at once.
- Custom discounts work even when no discount presets are set up.
- Print Bill is back for staff without reprint rights; a refused print says why.
- A second size or portion of an item no longer replaces the first.
- Opening a free table and going back no longer leaves an empty order.
- The desk's own reason is shown when it refuses a payment or a bill.
